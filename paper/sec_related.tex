\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Identifiability.}
Structural identifiability asks whether the parameters of a model can be determined from ideal outputs, and practical
identifiability asks the same of finite, noisy data
\citep[reviews in][]{cobelli1980parameter,miao2011identifiability,villaverde2016structural}; see also
\citet{bellman1970structural} and \citet{rothenberg1971identification}. \citet{chis2011structural} compare methods for
structural identifiability, and \citet{wieland2021structural} argue that practical identifiability is the harder question.
\citet{eisenberg2014determining} combine Fisher-information subset selection with the profile likelihood to find identifiable
parameter combinations, which is the question we ask of the gut rates (Section~\ref{sec:theory}), and
\citet{raue2009structural} use the profile likelihood to detect structural and practical non-identifiabilities. We use the
same instrument with fixed definitions of bounded, one-sided and flat intervals. The Fisher spectra of Section~\ref{sec:timing} spread over many
decades, as in sloppy-model analyses \citep{brown2003statistical,gutenkunst2007universally}. SIAN \citep{hong2019sian} and StructuralIdentifiability.jl
\citep{dong2023differential} implement structural analysis, and we run the second. \citet{tong2025invaert} invert a
haemodynamic model with a neural network although its parameters are structurally and practically non-identifiable. These
works supply instruments. We add controls that say whether a bounded or an unbounded interval can be believed: a replica in
which the model is true and a synthetic study with known truths.

\paragraph{Glucose--insulin models.}
The minimal model estimates an insulin sensitivity index from the glucose decay after an injection
\citep{bergman1979,bergman1981}, and nonlinear least squares often returns an insulin sensitivity of zero in type two
diabetes, which \citet{pillonetto2002minimal} address by Bayesian estimation. Meal simulation models describe the events after
a meal \citep{dallaman2007}, the UVA/Padova simulator has been extended for in silico trials \citep{dallaman2014uva}, and
\citet{cobelli2009diabetes} review the field. \citet{erdos2024leveraging} calibrate personalised models from CGM and plasma
insulin measured during an oral glucose tolerance test and report better practical identifiability with CGM glucose than with
plasma glucose. Their data come from a clinic with plasma insulin; ours are free-living meals without insulin, with the iAUC
and the trace as targets. Machine-learning models predict postprandial responses from meals and person-level features
\citep{zeevi2015personalized,berry2020human}, and GlucoBench collects public CGM datasets with forecasting benchmarks
\citep{sergazinov2024glucobench}. They do not fit a mechanistic simulator, so the question of what their parameters mean does
not arise for them. It arises for the twin.

\paragraph{Differentiable simulation, inference and digital twins.}
JAX and Diffrax provide differentiable numerical solvers \citep{bradbury2018jax,kidger2021diffrax}, and Jaxley uses them to
train detailed neuron models by gradient descent \citep{jaxley2025}. Gaussian ODE filtering gives estimators of the likelihood
gradient for ODE inverse problems \citep{kersting2020differentiable}, diffusion tempering eases gradient-based optimization where
nonlinear dynamics create many local minima \citep{beck2024diffusion}, a concern we share when we polish the trace fits
(Section~\ref{sec:more}), and \citet{liu2026ino} recover ODE parameters from sparse observations with a neural-operator
surrogate. Simulation-based inference learns posteriors with neural density estimators
\citep{greenberg2019automatic,tejerocantero2020sbi}, its behaviour under misspecification has been studied
\citep{ward2022robust,schmitt2023detecting}, and simulation-based calibration validates Bayesian inference
\citep{talts2018sbc}. Our replica validates profile intervals in the same spirit.

For diabetes twins, \citet{hoang2025t1dsbi} estimate parameters by neural posterior estimation, \citet{tran2026physioseq2seq}
combine matched physiological twins with a sequence model for glucose forecasting, and \citet{li2026glucofm} pretrain a CGM
foundation model for phenotype classification and postprandial-response prediction, tasks that do not ask for mechanistic
parameters. \citet{kuang2024medreal2sim} identify digital-twin parameters from non-invasive data by physics-informed
self-supervised learning, and HIPNO \citep{pan2026hipno} handles a scale symmetry of a haemodynamic observation model by
parameterizing a neural operator in the quotient space. Our symmetry is a discrete exchange of two rates in a mechanistic
simulator, and our question differs: we ask what the data determine about the parameters of a fitted twin, not how well the
twin predicts or how to avoid the ambiguity.
