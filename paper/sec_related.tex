\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Identifiability.}
Structural identifiability asks whether the parameters of a model can be determined from ideal outputs. Practical
identifiability asks the same of finite, noisy data
\citep[reviews in][]{cobelli1980parameter,miao2011identifiability,villaverde2016structural}; see also
\citet{bellman1970structural} and \citet{rothenberg1971identification}. \citet{chis2011structural} compare methods for
structural identifiability, and \citet{wieland2021structural} argue that practical identifiability is the harder
question. \citet{eisenberg2014determining} combine Fisher-information subset selection with the profile likelihood to
find identifiable parameter combinations, which is the question we ask of $\tau_1$ and $p$. \citet{raue2009structural} use the profile
likelihood to detect structural and practical non-identifiabilities and to derive confidence intervals. We use the
same instrument with fixed definitions of bounded, one-sided and flat intervals. Sloppy-model analyses find that the
sensitivity spectra of systems-biology models spread over many decades and that collective fits leave many
parameters poorly constrained \citep{brown2003statistical,gutenkunst2007universally}. The Fisher spectra of
Section~\ref{sec:timing} look the same. SIAN \citep{hong2019sian} and StructuralIdentifiability.jl
\citep{dong2023differential} implement structural analysis, and we run the second. \citet{tong2025invaert} use a
neural network to invert a lumped-parameter haemodynamic model whose parameters are structurally and practically
non-identifiable. These works supply instruments. We add controls that say whether a bounded or an unbounded interval
can be believed: a replica in which the model is true and a synthetic study with known truths.

\paragraph{Glucose--insulin models.}
The minimal model estimates an insulin sensitivity index from the glucose decay after an injection
\citep{bergman1979}, and was extended to give beta-cell responsivity and insulin sensitivity from an intravenous
glucose tolerance test \citep{bergman1981}. Nonlinear least squares in the MINMOD software often returns an
insulin sensitivity of zero in type two diabetes, and \citet{pillonetto2002minimal} address this by Bayesian
estimation. Meal simulation models describe the events after a meal \citep{dallaman2007}. The UVA/Padova type one
simulator has been extended for in silico trials \citep{dallaman2014uva}, and \citet{cobelli2009diabetes} review the
field. \citet{erdos2024leveraging} calibrate personalised models from CGM and plasma insulin measured during an oral
glucose tolerance test, and report that the practical identifiability of the parameters is better with CGM glucose
than with plasma glucose. Their data come from a clinic with plasma insulin. Ours are free-living meals without
insulin, and our target is the iAUC. Machine-learning models predict postprandial glycaemic responses from meals and
person-level features \citep{zeevi2015personalized,berry2020human}, and GlucoBench collects public CGM datasets with
forecasting benchmarks \citep{sergazinov2024glucobench}. These models do not fit a mechanistic simulator, so the
question of what their parameters mean does not arise for them. It arises for the twin.

\paragraph{Differentiable simulation, inference and digital twins.}
JAX and Diffrax provide differentiable numerical solvers \citep{bradbury2018jax,kidger2021diffrax}, and Jaxley uses
them to train detailed neuron models by gradient descent \citep{jaxley2025}. Gaussian ODE filtering gives estimators
of the likelihood gradient for ODE inverse problems \citep{kersting2020differentiable}. Diffusion tempering improves
the convergence of gradient-based parameter optimization where nonlinear dynamics create many local minima
\citep{beck2024diffusion}, a concern we share when we polish the trace fits (Section~\ref{sec:more}).
\citet{liu2026ino} recover ODE parameters from sparse observations with a neural-operator surrogate.

Simulation-based inference learns posteriors with neural density estimators
\citep{greenberg2019automatic,tejerocantero2020sbi}. Its behaviour under simulator misspecification has been studied
\citep{ward2022robust,schmitt2023detecting}, and simulation-based calibration is a procedure for validating Bayesian
inference \citep{talts2018sbc}. Our replica validates profile intervals in the same spirit.

For diabetes twins, \citet{hoang2025t1dsbi} estimate parameters by neural posterior estimation, and
\citet{tran2026physioseq2seq} combine matched physiological twins with a sequence model for glucose forecasting.
\citet{li2026glucofm} pretrain a CGM foundation model and evaluate it on phenotype classification and
postprandial-response prediction, tasks that do not ask for mechanistic parameters. \citet{kuang2024medreal2sim}
identify digital-twin parameters from non-invasive data by physics-informed self-supervised learning. HIPNO
\citep{pan2026hipno} handles a scale symmetry of a haemodynamic observation model by parameterizing a neural operator in
the quotient space of the symmetry. Our symmetry is a discrete exchange of two rates in a mechanistic simulator, and our
question differs. We ask what the data determine about the parameters of a fitted twin, not how well the twin predicts
or how to avoid the ambiguity.
