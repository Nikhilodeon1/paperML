Parameters of a mechanistic model fitted to wearable data are routinely read as physiology, and held-out
prediction is the usual evidence that this is safe.
We ask which parameters of a differentiable Bergman-type glucose--insulin simulator the incremental area
under the curve (iAUC) of postprandial continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) can determine, and whether
held-out iAUC prediction would reveal what it cannot.
We combine symbolic analysis, Fisher information, profile likelihood, a replica in which the model is true,
a synthetic study with known truths and cross-validated prediction on three cohorts (\resCohortCgmSubjects,
\resCohortShanghaiSubjects{} and \resCohortHallSubjects{} subjects).
Hypotheses \mbox{H1--H10} were fixed in version control before the analyses, and the rest are marked post hoc.
A single area carries little information about timing. The Fisher information of three parameters has median
eigenvalues of \resEigMedianOne, \resEigMedianTwo{} and \resEigMedianThree, and no profile interval for gastric
emptying or carbohydrate absorption is bounded in any subject, consistent with an exact exchange symmetry of the
empty-gut model.
Insulin sensitivity is bounded in \resProfileIaucSIInteriorBoundedOne{} of \resProfileIaucSIInteriorNOne{} subjects with
an interior estimate at the primary parameter box, in \resProfileIaucSIInteriorBoundedHalf{} of
\resProfileIaucSIInteriorNHalf{} at half the box and in \resProfileIaucSIInteriorBoundedTwo{} of
\resProfileIaucSIInteriorNTwo{} at twice the box. When the model is exactly true the figure is \resSeedsSIBounded\% over five replica seeds. On one seed it is
\resNoiseOneIaucSI\% at the estimated noise and \resNoiseQuarterIaucSI\% with the noise cut to a quarter.
Held-out iAUC error does not separate gradient fitting from grid search
(\resDiffGradThreeVsGridThree\ mg\,dL$^{-1}$\,min, $95\%$ interval \resDiffGradThreeVsGridThreeLow\ to
\resDiffGradThreeVsGridThreeHigh, on a mean error of \resMaeGradThree), and three fitted parameters match insulin
sensitivity alone within a margin of $150$ in two cohorts, so this prediction check does not reveal either failure.
Recovery of a combination of the two rates from the full glucose trace is not established. Polished fits bound it in
\resPolishedBoundedTauTrace\% and \resPolishedBoundedPTrace\% of real subjects and in \resReplicaCoordsTauTrace\%
and \resReplicaCoordsPTrace\% of true-model replicas, where the bounded intervals cover the truth in
\resReplicaCoordsCoverTauTrace\% and \resReplicaCoordsCoverPTrace\%. With exact carbohydrate the replica intervals are
calibrated and rarely bounded.
Of the planned hypotheses with a pass-or-fail rule, \resPlannedMet{} of \resPlannedGraded{} were met, and
\resPrimaryNotMet{} of the primary endpoints were not.
For this model, this target and these cohorts, a good fit to postprandial CGM is not evidence that the fitted
parameters mean what their names say. Code and frozen results are in the supplement.
