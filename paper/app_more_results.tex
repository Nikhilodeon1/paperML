\subsection{Further checks}

These analyses have no pass-or-fail rule; Appendix~\ref{app:register} records when each was declared.

\paragraph{Clustered intervals.}
The replica seeds reuse the same subjects, so the pooled fractions are clustered by subject. Resampling subjects, the
bounded fraction of $\SI$ under iAUC pooled over $5$ seeds is \resClustSI\% (\resClustSICount{} of \resClustSIPairs{}
subject--seed pairs, \resClustSISubjects{} subjects), with a $95\%$ interval of \resClustSILow\ to \resClustSIHigh\%.
For the polished trace coordinates pooled over seeds~$0$--$2$, $\tau_1$ is bounded in \resClustTau\%
(\resClustTauCount{} of \resClustTauPairs{}, interval \resClustTauLow\ to \resClustTauHigh\%) and $p$ in
\resClustP\% (\resClustPCount{} of \resClustPPairs{}, interval \resClustPLow\ to \resClustPHigh\%). The intervals
that ignore the clustering are \resClustSINaiveLow\ to \resClustSINaiveHigh\%, \resClustTauNaiveLow\ to
\resClustTauNaiveHigh\%, and \resClustPNaiveLow\ to \resClustPNaiveHigh\%.

\paragraph{Spacing of logged meals.}
The CGMacros logs contain \resOverlapGaps{} gaps between consecutive meals of a subject, with a median of
\resOverlapMedianGap{} hours. The share under three, four and six hours is \resOverlapWithinThree\% (Wilson interval
\resOverlapWithinThreeLow\ to \resOverlapWithinThreeHigh\%), \resOverlapWithinFour\% (\resOverlapWithinFourLow\ to
\resOverlapWithinFourHigh\%) and \resOverlapWithinSix\% (\resOverlapWithinSixLow\ to \resOverlapWithinSixHigh\%).
The model assumes an empty gut at each meal. We did not measure the residual content and so do not say how much of
the exchange symmetry this breaks.

\paragraph{Numerical checks.}
In the first \resTolSubjects{} subjects the Fisher information was recomputed at the stored estimates with a
relative solver tolerance of $10^{-8}$ in place of $10^{-4}$. The median relative change of an eigenvalue is
\resTolMedianRel\% for the smallest one, and the largest change, \resTolMaxRel\%, is in a subject whose smallest
eigenvalue is at numerical zero. The absolute cosine of the weak direction with the exchange direction is at least
\resTolMinCos{} in every subject. For the same subjects, \resStepsProfiles{} profiles were recomputed with $500$ inner
optimizer steps in place of $100$, and \resStepsChanged{} changed class (bounded, one-sided or flat).

\paragraph{A trivial proxy for HbA1c.}
In the Shanghai subjects of Appendix~\ref{apx:leakage} (\resProxyN{} with HbA1c), the iAUC divided by the logged
carbohydrate has a Pearson correlation of \resProxyRaw{} (\resProxyRawLow{} to \resProxyRawHigh) with HbA1c and
\resProxyPartial{} (\resProxyPartialLow{} to \resProxyPartialHigh) with fasting glucose partialled out. This model-free ratio provides a baseline comparison for the raw correlation of the amortized estimate.

\paragraph{Cutoff, systematic error, and meal-clustered sandwich adjustment.}
A cutoff that covers the truth for $90\%$ of subjects needs a noise standard deviation
\resCutQuarterSigmaTau{} times larger at $0.25$ and \resCutThreeQuarterSigmaTau{} times larger at $0.75$ for $\tau_1$, and at
those cutoffs only \resCutQuarterBoundedTau\% and \resCutThreeQuarterBoundedTau\% of interior subjects are bounded. A systematic
error, one multiplier per subject shared by every meal with coefficient of variation $0.25$, breaks the trace coordinate intervals as well:
they are bounded for \resSysTauBounded\% and \resSysPBounded\% of interior subjects and contain the truth for \resSysTauCover\%
and \resSysPCover\%. Unlike the iAUC objective (where a constant carbohydrate scale is absorbed largely by $\SI$, Appendix~\ref{apx:si}), under the full trace nonlinear insulin feedback and the fixed baseline prevent $\SI$ alone from absorbing a uniform load scale without distorting curve shape, shifting $(\hat\tau_1,\hat p)$ and breaking interval coverage.

Table~\ref{tab:sandwich} summarizes the meal-clustered Huber--White sandwich adjustment (H42, \resVerdictHFortyTwo) on replica seed~$0$. With meal-level score vectors $g_m = J_m^\top r_m$ and Gauss--Newton Hessian $H = \sum_{m=1}^M J_m^\top J_m$ at the polished trace optimum $\hat\phi$, we form $\hat V_{\mathrm{sand}} = \frac{M}{M-3} H^{-1} (\sum_{m=1}^M g_m g_m^\top) H^{-1}$ and rescale each coordinate's profile threshold by $c_j = \max(1, \hat V_{\mathrm{sand},jj} / (H^{-1})_{jj})$.

\paragraph{Replication of the carbohydrate-error sweep.}
For $\tau_1$ the bounded share rises from \resSeedOneZeroTauBounded\% and \resSeedTwoZeroTauBounded\% at exact carbohydrate to
\resSeedOneHighTauBounded\% and \resSeedTwoHighTauBounded\% at $0.75$ on replica seeds~$1$ and~$2$, while coverage falls from
\resSeedOneZeroTauCover\% and \resSeedTwoZeroTauCover\% to \resSeedOneHighTauCover\% and \resSeedTwoHighTauCover\%. On the replica
built from the \resShanghaiN{} Shanghai subjects the $\tau_1$ bounded share rises from \resShanghaiRepZeroTauBounded\% to
\resShanghaiRepHighTauBounded\% and coverage falls from \resShanghaiRepZeroTauCover\% to \resShanghaiRepHighTauCover\%. For $p$ the bounded share rises by \resSeedOneRiseP{} and \resSeedTwoRiseP{} points between exact carbohydrate and a logging
error of $0.75$ on replica seeds~$1$ and~$2$, while coverage falls by \resSeedOneDropP{} and \resSeedTwoDropP{} points. On the
Shanghai replica the bounded share of $p$ rises from \resShanghaiRepZeroPBounded\% to \resShanghaiRepHighPBounded\% and
coverage falls from \resShanghaiRepZeroPCover\% to \resShanghaiRepHighPCover\%. On the real Shanghai data the trace intervals
for $\tau_1$ and $p$ are bounded for \resShanghaiTraceTauBounded\% and \resShanghaiTracePBounded\% of interior subjects and
the iAUC interval for $\SI$ for \resShanghaiIaucSIBounded\% (\resShanghaiIaucSIBoundedCount{} of
\resShanghaiIaucSIBoundedN); only \resShanghaiIaucKeBoundedN{} and \resShanghaiIaucKaBoundedN{} subjects are interior for
$\ke$ and $\ka$, too few to report a fraction.
