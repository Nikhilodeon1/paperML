## 2. T2: well-specified replica (H13, H14)

Truth: each subject's regularized prediction-fit estimate, moved at least 5% inside the 1x box; real meal schedules;
AR(1) CGM noise whose variance and lag-1 correlation come from that subject's real residuals (fallback sigma 10,
rho 0.7); lognormal carbohydrate logging error with unit mean and CV c; independent seeded streams for CGM and
carbohydrate error.

**H13 (rule: S_I bounded in >= 50% of interior subjects, k_e and k_a bounded in <= 25%; replica, 1x box, c = 0.25,
CGM noise on).**
S_I bounded in 9 of 34 interior subjects (0.265, Wilson 0.146 to 0.431); 9 of 45 overall (0.20). k_e and k_a:
0 of 45 each. 7 of the 9 bounded S_I intervals contain the true value (0.78). Verdict **not met** (S_I clause fails,
timing clause holds). Reading recorded by the code: mixed. In words: even when the model is exactly true and the
noise is the stated noise, the S_I interval is bounded for about a quarter of interior subjects, which is about twice
the real-data rate at the same box (3 of 22 = 0.14) and far below the 0.5 rule; the timing parameters are never
bounded, as the exchange symmetry predicts. So the weakness of the S_I interval is largely a property of the
observable and the design, with the real-data rate lower still. The coverage of 0.78 for the 9 bounded intervals is
below the nominal 0.95; with 9 intervals this is not estimated precisely.

**H14 (reported, no pass/fail; ratio of replica to real held-out iAUC MAE, grad3, 3 repeats of five-fold, 45 subjects;
real MAE 3,024.7 mg/dL*min).**

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CGM noise off, carbohydrate error 0 | 1.86 | 0.0006 | 0.0003 to 0.0011 |

Primary ratio 0.836: between 0.7 and 0.9, so the pre-stated reading is **mixed**. Grid-trained fits on the replica
match the gradient fits to within about 3 mg/dL*min in every setting (for example 2,531.2 against 2,528.3 at the

## 8. Failures and what they say (reported, not repaired)

H3, H5, H9, H11, H12, H13 are **not met**. The pattern is consistent: richer observables (trace) bound the timing
parameters in rate space only at a wider box; coordinates make them bounded when the trace is used (H11) but not with the
centroid; the tied model predicts as well as the three-parameter model (H12, first clause) without identifying tau1; and a
true-model replica does not bound S_I for most subjects (H13), so the S_I weakness is not mainly a misspecification
artifact. H14 is mixed: noise and carbohydrate error together explain about 0.84 of the real held-out error under the
assumed noise model, and the CGM-noise setting is an upper bound.

## 9. Missing and caveats the paper must state

* T5(iii), H10, A11, A15 and Dalla Man were not run; the "two model classes" claim is unsupported in this revision.
* Shanghai 2x profile: 85 of 87 subjects.
* Replica CGM noise comes from real-fit residuals (includes misfit); the replica truth is a fit, not a ground truth.
* Interior-subject counts for k_e and k_a at 1x are 2 to 5 in Shanghai and Hall.
* The sign of the stored carbohydrate-scale reference value was wrong in the Phase 8 summary and is corrected (section 5).
* The abstract and TL;DR text has not been supplied, so ledger section A labels the five known suspect claims by name only.
