\section{Hypothesis register and amendments}
\label{app:register}

The analysis plan fixed ten hypotheses (H1--H10) and their thresholds in version control before any analysis was run. It
was amended \resAmendmentCount{} times, and no amendment changed a threshold of an earlier document or edited an earlier
verdict; later analyses add rows. Table~\ref{tab:register} gives every hypothesis with its rule, its origin and the status
computed from the stored results. Of the \resPlannedGraded{} planned hypotheses with a pass-or-fail rule \resPlannedMet{}
were met, and of the \resLaterGraded{} graded hypotheses added later (H11 onward) \resLaterMet{} were met. Of the five primary endpoints of the plan, \resPrimaryNotMet{} were not met, \resPrimaryMet{} was met and H1 is
formulation-dependent.

\paragraph{Where the plan and the record differ.}
The plan stated H5 as a rule over subjects ``interior-converged'', which is empty here (amendment~$2$). Its H1 rule, to
delete the exchange framing if the rates were globally identifiable, fired under the formulation the plan specified, which
assumed a generic nonzero intestinal content; the simulator starts every meal with an empty intestine, where the symmetry is
exact, so we keep the framing and the register reports H1 as formulation-dependent. An error decomposition of an earlier
analysis, which had no definition under which its terms added, was not carried forward; the replica noise budgets replace it.

\paragraph{Protocol notes.}
The Shanghai external-generalization check (Appendix~\ref{apx:leakage}) reports Pearson correlations on subjects with at least $3$ meals rather than the $10$-meal primary filter. Every bibliography entry was verified against Crossref, arXiv, or publisher pages, and every numerical claim in the manuscript is generated programmatically from stored result files.

\section{Analyses not run}
\label{app:notrun}

A design sweep over meal size and timing, a Dalla Man-on-Dalla-Man replica or full-trace Dalla Man study, heteroskedastic noise models
for the trace objective, a day-continuous replica, a liquid-versus-solid meal positive control, and a latent-carbohydrate hierarchical likelihood were not run. An analysis of clinical correlations of $\SI$ across cohorts
was not repeated; only the Shanghai check of Appendix~\ref{apx:leakage} is reported.
